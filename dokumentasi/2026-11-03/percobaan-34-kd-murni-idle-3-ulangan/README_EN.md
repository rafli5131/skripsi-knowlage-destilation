# Experiment 34 — Pure KD idle

Three Pure KD seed-42 idle repetitions were completed with an SSH-X preview and without robot motion commands. All three passed automated CSV and sampled telemetry checks; final protocol review remains open.

Mean ± sample SD: latency **74.09 ± 1.04 ms**, effective FPS **12.07 ± 0.05**, process CPU **1300.21 ± 2.44% of one logical CPU**, RSS **356.97 ± 5.92 MiB**. There were 2,172 measured frames with no frame errors; model output coverage is not labeled accuracy. Direct torque readout was unavailable while the manager owned the bus; external scene photographs, measured distances, before/after raw reference frames, and immediate post-run CPU temperatures were not collected.

See the [Indonesian summary](README_ID.md), [three-run table](05-ringkasan/tabel_3_run.csv), [English HD graph](04-kd-murni/graph_kd42_idle_repetitions_1_2_3_hd.png), and per-run folders. The campaign has 21/24 slots passing automated checks; three Pure KD walking slots remain.
