# Experiment 33 — Supervised walking

Three Supervised seed-1337 walking repetitions were completed with operator confirmation before each gait, an SSH-X preview, and no head position commands. All gait starts and stops were acknowledged; maximum head deviation from the guard baseline was one encoder count. Automated CSV and telemetry checks passed for all three, with final protocol audit still open.

Across the three runs, mean ± sample SD was **70.80 ± 2.71 ms latency**, **12.64 ± 0.52 FPS**, **1301.13 ± 6.04% process CPU of one logical CPU**, and **359.16 ± 4.76 MiB RSS**. There were 2,275 measured frames and zero frame errors. Detection output coverage is not labeled accuracy.
